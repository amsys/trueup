# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in this project is expected to treat other people
with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, personal attacks, or harassment of any kind.
- Sharing another person's private information without their consent.
- Any conduct that would be unreasonable in a professional setting.

## Reporting a problem

Report a problem to the maintainer's contact address in the README. Every
report will be looked at and answered.

## Enforcement

A maintainer who violates this code may face the same consequences as
anyone else.
