# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security problem. Instead, send a report
to the maintainer's contact address in the README, with:

- The steps to reproduce the problem.
- What an attacker could do with it.
- The affected version or commit.

The maintainer will reply within a reasonable time and let you know the
next step.
